Delete your Om Sai Sannidhi account

Om Sai Sannidhi is published by [developer name — set before publishing].

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Om Sai Sannidhi and sign in.
  2. If you own a reading group, open the group and transfer ownership to another member first.
  3. On the home screen choose Delete account and confirm.

If you signed in more than five minutes ago, the app asks you to log out and log in again before deleting. This protects your account from being deleted by someone else using your device.

Or ask us to delete it

If you cannot use the app, email [contact email — set before publishing] from the address you registered with, or tell us the phone number you signed in with. We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email, phone number, or Google or Apple link).
  • Your private profile and reminder preferences.
  • Your group memberships and any group-visible name you published.

What is kept

  • Messages, highlights, reading progress and reports you already shared in a group stay there under an anonymous ID without your name, so the group's history remains complete. Group admins can remove reported messages and highlights.
  • If a group was archived by its admin, your anonymous membership entry stays until the group is restored.
  • Bookmarks and settings stored on your phone stay until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
