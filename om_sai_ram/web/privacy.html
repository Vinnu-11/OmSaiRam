Om Sai Sannidhi helps devotees read Shri Sai Satcharita, alone or in reading groups. This page explains what the app stores and why. Contact: [developer name and contact email — set before publishing].

What we store

Data Why Who can see it
Sign-in details: email address, phone number, or your Google or Apple account identifier, depending on how you sign in. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. To sign you in and keep your account yours. Only you and the service.
Private display name and reminder preferences. To personalise the app. Only you.
Group membership and role, and a group-visible name if you choose to publish one. To run reading groups. Members of that group.
Reading assignments and progress, chat messages, highlights and comments, and reports you submit. To share reading with your group and keep it safe. Members of that group. Reports are visible to you and the group admin.

Stored only on your device and never sent to us: bookmarks, saved reading position, language and font settings, device reminder settings and downloaded audio.

The reading text needs no account. We do not show advertising, sell data, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Services we rely on

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Hosting) stores the account and group data above.
  • Google or Apple sign-in, if you choose it, is handled by Google or Apple. Phone sign-in sends you an SMS code.
  • Audio files are downloaded from GitHub, the live darshan stream comes from its official source, and fonts are downloaded from Google Fonts. Those services receive your network address when the app uses them.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Delete account on the home screen) or by following the account deletion instructions.

Deleting your account removes your sign-in, private profile and reminder preferences and takes you out of your groups. Messages, highlights, reading progress and reports you already shared stay in those groups under an anonymous ID without your name, so the group's history remains complete. Admins can remove reported messages and highlights.

Deactivating an account is different: it pauses the account and keeps all data until you restore or delete it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date above.
